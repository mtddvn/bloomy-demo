Play 10 minutes

Stack, wait, topple

You'll need: 4–6 blocks or stacking cups, and a floor you don't mind being noisy

Saved to Mia's memories — you can find it in Growth, under This week.

How to play

  1. Sit facing Mia and stack two blocks slowly, so she can watch your hands.
  2. Pause with your hand hovering — let her knock it down or add one herself.
  3. Rebuild together, counting out loud, “one… two…”.
  4. When she stacks two, stop and celebrate that, not the tower's height.
  5. Finish by toppling the tallest tower together, on purpose, with a countdown.

Shorter today · 5 minutes

Skip the counting. Stack, wait for her hands, topple — three towers is a whole game.

When it clicks

Trying looks like Mia stacking two, watching them fall, and handing you the next block. That's the game.
